## 2. ipSAE: why `of3_ipsae` is 0 and what replaces it

`ipsae` is Dunbrack's d0res variant at the standard 10 A PAE cutoff. It is exactly 0 whenever the best VHH-HER2 PAE is above 10 A (the trastuzumab control
has `min_ipae` 10.2 A), and its d0 is only about 1-2 A for small residue counts, so even at 15/20 A cutoffs it stays near 0.01-0.02 (`ipsae15`, `ipsae20`).
The implementation is right (cand01 of the earlier Fv set gives 0.36 with `min_ipae` 2.0); the metric is simply flat for weak interfaces.
Added: `ipsae_chn10` / `ipsae_chn15` (Dunbrack's chain-length d0, about 5-6 A, 10 / 15 A cutoff): the trastuzumab control scores 0.23 at 15 A against a median of
0.07 (max 0.15) for the 12 framework-only decoys; and `ipae_tm` = mean over contacting pairs of 1/(1+(PAE/8 A)^2), no cutoff. The a-priori OF3 consensus
(`of3_consensus_rank_score`) is the mean percentile of **ipsae_chn15, ipae_tm, min_ipae (lower better), iptm** among rule-passing designs, all equal weight;
chosen before looking at design scores, so it is not tuned on them.

## 3. Calibration (100 labelled complexes, same pipeline as designs; `calibration.md`)

* Fv-SPR (binders KD <= 10 nM vs no reportable KD, 12/12): OF3 ipae_tm 0.80; OpenDDE-abag min_ipae 0.69; ESMFold-2 min_ipae 0.76 (all CIs span 0.5-0.9 for 12 vs 12).
* Fv-FACS (high vs low, 16/16, 4-6 changes): every scorer is at or **below chance** (OF3 ipae_tm 0.33, OpenDDE iptm 0.16): the model is more confident about FACS-low variants.
  Take this as a warning, not a verdict: these are saturated near-trastuzumab Fv sequences, not de novo VHHs.
* VHH-antigen crystal pairs, cognate vs shuffled (10/34): OF3 AUC 0.54-0.65, OpenDDE-abag 0.65-0.72, ESMFold-2 0.61-0.64; cognate vs the shuffled pairs AlphaFold3
  scored highest (10/12): 0.37-0.54 for all (no better than chance). This reproduces Smorodina et al. 2026: confidence does not tell real from shuffled VHH-antigen pairs.
* Gates are the 95th percentile of the decoy pool (shuffled pairs + lysozyme decoys + framework-only VHHs; 46-77 values) on a primary metric **fixed a priori**
  (OF3 and ESMFold-2: `ipsae_chn15`; OpenDDE-abag: `min_ipae`; AF2-IG: `interface_pae`). 13 candidate metrics on 10 positives would only select noise, so the best-AUC metric is
  reported but not used. Each gate passes only 20-40% of the 10 cognate crystal pairs and **no rule-passing RFD4 design passes any gate** (the decoy pool includes the shuffled pairs the
  models are confidently wrong about), so `n_models_pass` is 0 for designs; use `consensus_rank_score` / `of3_consensus_rank_score` for ordering.
* No HER2-specific VHH positives were available (sequences of the published HER2 VHHs were not retrievable in time) and the organisers' 454 negatives are not in the bucket
  (only four public folders). The Smorodina pairs are the stand-in; they are not HER2.

## 4. What we trust

* OF3 `ipae_tm` / `min_ipae` and OpenDDE-abag `min_ipae` as weak ordering signals **within** the rule-passing RFD4 designs; nothing as a binding call.
* The competition proxy (`epi_overlap`, `clash30`, `cdr_fv_frac4`) as geometry of the predicted pose, only for designs whose pose is stable across samples (`pose_rmsd_max`) and seeds
  (`of3_seed_*`). Most RFD4 designs have pose RMSD of tens of Angstrom across OF3 samples: the model does not converge on one interface, so overlap numbers are weak.
* Agreement across independent models (`xmodel_n_agree`, `n_models_pass`) over any single model. With gates this strict `n_models_pass` is mostly 0; rank by
  `consensus_rank_score` and look at `xmodel_*` for the shortlist.
* Not trusted: ipTM alone, pLDDT, Boltz-2, and **ESMFold-2 and OpenDDE-abag for specificity**: both are as confident on the shuffled pairs AlphaFold3 scored highest
  (median `ipae_tm` 0.94 and 0.91, `min_ipae` 0.66 / 0.45 A) as on the cognate crystal pairs (0.86 / 0.91, 0.70 / 0.52 A), i.e. blind to specificity on this set (and the crystal
  pairs are probably in their training data). OF3 is the least fooled by the hard shuffled pairs (median `ipae_tm` 0.20 vs 0.14 for cognate: no separation either).

## 5. Observations on the real designs (rfd4jf, rule-passing, OF3)

* OF3 sees almost no confident interface: iptm median 0.11-0.14 per (framework, hotspot) group, max 0.54; `ipsae_chn15` medians 0.07-0.15 against decoys 0.07 (max 0.15);
  designs above the decoy maximum exist in every group (best: bcii10 / hs3_alt_patch). This is the expected outcome for a no-template docking test (see section 1) and
  must not be read as "the designs are bad" or "the designs bind".
* OpenDDE-abag is more confident on the designs (tier-2 designs: median `min_ipae` 5.1 A, `iptm` 0.30) than OF3 (14.2 A, 0.11), above the framework-only decoys (7.4 A, max 9.6 A) and
  near the trastuzumab control (4.7 A), but far from the cognate crystal pairs (0.5 A). ESMFold-2: designs median `ipae_tm` 0.16 (max 0.72) vs decoys 0.16 (max 0.25).
* Lysozyme decoy (OF3, tier-2 designs): median `iptm` 0.20 vs 0.11 against HER2: designs are, if anything, more confident against lysozyme than against HER2 (the decoy has no
  HER2-style MSA signal, and docking confidence is not specific); the specificity delta is reported but should not be used as a gate.
* Per-generator breakdown is in `calibration.md` ("Per generator"); only `rfd4jf` and the controls remain after BoltzGen and fwdiv were dropped.

## 6. Operational choices

* Everything is resumable and restart-safe: jobs are named by a content hash, cached, and adopted again after a restart (`adopt()`); parsed metrics are versioned (`PARSE_V`) and
  `reparse` recomputes them from stored structures at no API cost (used four times when metrics were added).
* Budget: stage cap $25. Scoring is cheap (OF3 about $0.0025 per target); the wall-clock limit is the JapanFold account queue, which serialises our jobs.
* Bugs found and fixed on the way (kept here because they would bite a re-run): `designs_boltzgen_metrics.csv` matches the `designs_*.csv` glob and has no `generator` column (S3 filters
  such rows; `dn_common.load_designs` still returns them); `results.json` in a JapanFold archive is a list, S3 stores its own copy as `s3_results.json`.
