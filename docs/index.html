The challenge

One target, two tracks.

Bind HER2 domain IV where trastuzumab binds. Either make trastuzumab better, or design something new from scratch. The organisers' lab measures the winners after the event: affinity, epitope preservation and thermostability.

Target

HER2 domain IV, UniProt P04626 residues 489–607.

Reference

Trastuzumab Fv bound to HER2 (PDB 1N8Z). 19 HER2 residues contact it.

Optimization track

Improve trastuzumab, in trastuzumab’s own format (paired VH + VL).

Optimization track

CDR-H3 + Vernier redesign of trastuzumab

Start from the best-tested CDR-H3 in the provided SPR data (WGSQGFYAYD: 0.023 nM, one replicate; trastuzumab 0.114 nM, eight replicates). Change 3–4 of the six positions that do not touch HER2 with a BLOSUM-45 recipe, add conservative Vernier-zone swaps where they touch a changed loop position, and keep only designs that dock like the crystal.

Only OpenDDE antibody–antigen put both measured binders above both measured non-binders, so it is the only fold model used. OpenFold3 with an MSA was flat (about 0.5); ESMFold-2-fast scored the non-binders above trastuzumab.

How a design is judged

  • Same epitope. Superpose on the 1N8Z Fv framework; the 19 epitope residues must sit within 3 Å.
  • Same contacts. GetContacts residue-pair comparison with the crystal: 27 Fv–HER2 pairs, 6 with CDR-H3, plus 22 CDR-H3-to-framework pairs.
  • Salt bridge. The R98 (H94) – position 10 bridge is kept, or both partners must change.
  • Rank. iLIS first, ipSAE as tie-break (both are our re-implementations).
Caveats. One seed per complex, so the top of the list is within noise (ipTM jumps between about 0.6 and 0.95). No metric here predicts affinity. The salt-bridge rule keeps three designs without the bridge whose partners are both mutated; that compensation is a hypothesis. abguard is the organisers’ demo build; the jury’s checker may differ.
De novo track

Two approaches submitted, one explored

Three independent pipelines designed new HER2 binders against the trastuzumab epitope, all generating with RFD4-Proteina but differing in scaffold, gates and ranking. Two of them are submitted as de novo VHHs: 7 designs from Approach A and 8 from Approach B. The third is shown as explored work.

A Approach A

RFD4-Proteina VHH

A gate-first pipeline designs all three CDRs of a nanobody on an intact framework, then filters, scores and ranks by Pareto dominance.

  • 1,544 unique designs on two VHH frameworks (814 caplacizumab-like, 730 hNbBCII10-FGLA).
  • Hard gates: CDR3 of 12–18 aa, no unpaired Cys, canonical disulfide, structural validity, no clash with the unresolved HER2 loop 581–590, abguard de novo class with a 2-point margin.
  • Scored by OpenFold3 (HER2 template, no MSA) and ESMFold-2; OpenDDE on a cropped HER2 (539–607) as a gate against framework-only decoys.
  • Profiled for developability (TNP), melting temperature (NanoMelt) and yield (ESMC-Nb monoyield plus nine CDR rules).
  • 43 designs pass every gate; Pareto over binding, TNP, yield and Tm gives a top 20.
Honest read. No predictor reproduced the designed binding poses (OpenDDE median 44 Å away, 26 of 768 within 20 Å; OpenFold3 none of 571 within 20 Å), and all 20 listed designs carry the below-decoy flag. A filter, not evidence of binding.

B Approach B

Caplacizumab-framework VHH

RFD4-Proteina designs the three CDRs of a VHH on the caplacizumab framework, aimed at the trastuzumab epitope; two folding models with a HER2 template pick the survivors.

  • 120 designs from three seeds; 118 pass the local gate and the organisers’ checker (class de novo, identity to trastuzumab 0.63–0.70).
  • Refolded with Boltz-2 and OpenFold3-OpenBind (templated), ranked by the min-iPAE percentile of both.
  • Kept only if the refold lands within 10 Å of the designed pose with at least 0.2 epitope overlap; one design per CDR3 cluster.
  • Calibrated first on 25 variants with measured KD: Boltz-2 Spearman −0.41 (orders loosely, not reliably).
  • The approach delivered 6 designs in total.
Honest read. Only 2 of 120 designs pass the organisers’ refold oracle in at least one model, and there is no lab data. Ranking separates clearly good from clearly bad, not a precise order.

C Approach C · also explored

Data-trained CDR-H3 on the Fv

RFD4-Proteina co-generates a new CDR-H3 on the trastuzumab Fv; classifiers trained on the measured screens and template-aware refolding pick the winners.

  • Binder classifier (LightGBM) trained on the 524k-variant FACS screen: test AUC 0.994 on held-out sequence families; extrapolation to loops far from the screen is less certain.
  • Refolded with Boltz-2 and Protenix-v2, ranked on min-iPAE plus binder pLDDT because ipTM saturates; trastuzumab’s own CDR-H3 lands on the 1N8Z epitope at 2.17 Å as a positive control.
  • A shortlist of 15 designs.
Why it is not in the de novo list. Only CDR-H3 is new and the trastuzumab framework stays (about 97 % identical to the trastuzumab VH). The organisers’ checker classes these designs as ‘novel’, the optimisation type rather than de novo, and rejected 10 of the 15 shortlisted candidates for CDR-H3 novelty.
